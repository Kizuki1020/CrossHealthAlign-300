## Key metric definitions

**Over-Alignment Rate (OA)**  
Non-equivalent items (RELATED_NOT_EQUIVALENT or NO_JUSTIFIED_EQUIVALENT)
predicted as EQUIVALENT, divided by the number of non-equivalent items.

**Appropriate Rejection Rate (ARR)**  
Gold NO_JUSTIFIED_EQUIVALENT items predicted as NO_JUSTIFIED_EQUIVALENT,
divided by gold NO_JUSTIFIED_EQUIVALENT count.

**Expected Calibration Error (ECE)**  
10 equal-width confidence bins on [0, 1].

**Macro-F1 (3-class)**  
Unweighted mean of F1 over EQUIVALENT, RELATED_NOT_EQUIVALENT, and
NO_JUSTIFIED_EQUIVALENT only.
